# AI assistance and human responsibility

This research artifact was developed with substantial assistance from OpenAI Codex for literature-search support, planning, code generation, test generation, documentation, and review.

AI systems are tools, not authors. They do not accept responsibility for the work. Prathamesh Kalamkar directed the project and must review, understand, reproduce, and approve every configuration, experiment, claim, citation, and public submission made under his name.

The following safeguards apply:

- no generated numerical result is treated as evidence without an executed, preserved experiment record;
- no citation is accepted without checking the original source and relevant version;
- all authors and contributors must describe their actual contribution accurately;
- venue-specific AI disclosure, authorship, anonymity, ethics, and artifact rules take precedence;
- this file will be updated when the model-assisted workflow materially changes.
